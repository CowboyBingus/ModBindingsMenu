Mod Bindings Menu v2.2
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer (v19 is current) and import this
ZIP into Arsenal or HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2.
Keep the shared loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.

Open Options > Mouse & Keyboard or Controller and press E (or RB) to reach the
MODS tab. Each installed mod has its own section there. Bindings can use any
activation type (Press, Hold, Double Tap, Long Press...) and controller buttons.
Up to 36 mod bindings are available; two mods may share a key.

Install Ship Station Hotkeys (formerly Galactic Menu Hotkey) separately, or
enable its Vanilla Plus Megapack option, to rebind its six shortcuts.
Vanilla Plus Megapack also bundles Mod Bindings Menu as an option:
use either that option or this standalone addon, not both.

The MODS tab follows the game's Text Language when a translation is
installed; see TRANSLATING.md in the repository.

Upgrading from v1: your saved keys are kept. The MODS section moves from the
General tab to the MODS tab.

Rebuild the input resource after game updates. Conflicts with other
content/input.config replacements or mods using the Freeflight,
CinematicCamera, DebugAvatar or Debugmenu input actions.
